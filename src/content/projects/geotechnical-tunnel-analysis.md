This undergraduate geotechnical project focused on deformation and support analysis of headrace tunnels located across three tectonic zones of Nepal. The study title identifies an empirical and numerical approach to the analysis.

## Tectonic zones considered

The project considered the Siwalik, Lower Himalaya and Higher Himalaya zones.

## Study approach

The work examined tunnel deformation and support analysis using empirical and numerical approaches. Detailed methods, personal contributions and project outputs should be added only where they can be verified from the original project report.

## Recognition

The project received the **Best Undergraduate Geotechnical Project Award** from the Nepal Geotechnical Society in 2025.
